Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Decoded app for iPhone, iPad and Android (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

What stays on your device

The puzzles you've solved, your favorites, times, streaks, achievements and settings are stored on your device. The Home Screen widget reads the same data from your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

iCloud

A copy of your progress is kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it stays the same on your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see it.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer only sees scores as they appear on the public leaderboards.

Quote cards you share

Quote images are only created when you tap Share, and only go where you send them.

Android

On Android, your progress, streaks and library are stored only on your phone. There’s no iCloud or Game Center. If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include this data in your own Google account backup, which the developer can’t see. Quote cards you share go only where you choose to send them. The app has no internet access.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
